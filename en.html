Ocho Puertas Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Ocho Puertas is a tower defense game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

In short: Ocho Puertas does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no usage analytics and no user accounts, and it works offline.

Controller

The controller is the developer of Ocho Puertas, Madelato Software (Spain). Contact: madelatosoftware@gmail.com.

What is stored and where

The game stores on your device, in the app's private folder:

  • Your saved games: progress, seals, upgrades bought, enemies discovered and chosen difficulty.
  • Your settings: graphics quality and frame rate limit.

This data never leaves your device: neither the developer nor anyone else can see it.

Permissions

The game does not request any special Android permission: no Internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Google Play

Downloads, installation and updates are handled by Google Play. Google may provide the developer with aggregated, anonymous statistics (number of installs, system crash reports) under its own privacy policy. The game itself sends nothing.

How to delete your data

  • You can delete a saved game from the game's start screen ("Delete").
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything the game had stored.

We keep no other data about you.

Children

Ocho Puertas is not directed at children under 13. The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes any of the above, this page and its date will be updated before that version is published.
