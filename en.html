Ocho Puertas Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Ocho Puertas is a tower defense game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

In short:

  • Your saved games and settings stay on your phone.
  • The game can send anonymous usage statistics, but only if you accept: we ask the first time and you can change it any time in Settings. If you say no, nothing is sent.
  • The statistics do not include your name, your email, your location or anything you type. They carry a random identifier that does not identify you.
  • There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no user accounts.

Controller

The controller is the developer of Ocho Puertas, Madelato Software (Spain). Contact: madelatosoftware@gmail.com.

What is stored on your phone

In the app's private folder: your saved games (progress, seals, upgrades bought, enemies discovered and chosen difficulty), your settings and your answer to the statistics question. If you accept, the random identifier, the install day, the attempt counters per level and difficulty and the marks of the level in progress (to report an abrupt close) are stored too. Saved games and settings never leave your phone. You can delete a saved game from the start screen ("Delete") and everything is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Anonymous statistics (only if you accept)

What is sent. Each event carries the name of the game, its version and a random identifier created on your installation (it is not your advertising ID or your device ID). Depending on what you do in the game:

  • when the game opens: language, phone model, Android version, the graphics quality, graphics engine (Normal or Compatibility) and frame rate limit, and whether it is the first time and the install day;
  • when you accept the statistics: that you accepted and from where (first launch or Settings);
  • in the intro: whether you skipped it and how many seconds you watched;
  • in each level: which one, the difficulty and the attempt number; how it ended (won, lost, left or closed), stars and wave reached, gate health and gold left, how long you played, which towers you built, upgraded or sold, which enemies damaged the gate, how many times you used abilities and how smoothly the game ran (frames per second);
  • the upgrades you buy in the skill tree and the settings you change (and to what value);
  • whether you open "Suggestions and contact" and whether your email app could be opened (not the content of the email);
  • game errors: where in the code they happened and a short message, without file paths from your phone (at most 5 per session);
  • if the game closed abruptly last time: the last level and difficulty.

What is not sent. Your name, your email, your location, your contacts, photos, or anything you type. The game asks PostHog not to store your IP address or infer your location, and it does not create profiles of people.

What for. To know how many people play, where they get stuck and what fails, so we can balance the game and fix errors. It is not used for advertising and it is not sold.

Legal basis. Your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time, and withdrawing does not affect what was sent before.

Processor. The events are received and stored by PostHog (PostHog Inc., a US company), acting as our processor. We use its European region: according to PostHog, data of customers who choose EU hosting is stored and processed on servers in Germany (PostHog's privacy policy). For transfers outside the EU, PostHog's processor agreement (DPA) provides for the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, and PostHog states in its policy that it is part of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. We do not share them with anyone else.

How long. Events are kept for PostHog's retention period for our plan (currently 1 year, according to posthog.com/pricing); after that they are deleted automatically. On your phone, events wait in a queue (up to 500) until they can be sent.

How to withdraw. In Settings, "Anonymous statistics: No". The game stops sending and deletes the pending events and the identifier from your phone. If you accept again later, a new identifier is created.

How to ask for what was already sent to be deleted

Write to madelatosoftware@gmail.com with your anonymous identifier: it is at the bottom of the "Send a suggestion" and "Report a problem" emails (if you have accepted the statistics). If you already said no, the identifier was deleted from your phone and we cannot tell which events are yours; they will be deleted when they expire.

Suggestions and contact

From Settings, "Suggestions and contact" opens your email app with a message prepared for madelatosoftware@gmail.com. The game does not send it: you do, and you can edit or delete it first. At the bottom it carries the game version, your phone and Android model, the language, the last level played and, if you accepted the statistics, your anonymous identifier. The game does not collect your address or what you write; but if you write to us from your email app, we will receive your address and your message, and we will use them only to reply to you.

Permissions

The game requests the Internet permission, only to send the statistics if you accept. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or files. Opening the email or this policy is done by your email app or your browser.

Google Play

Downloads, installation and updates are handled by Google Play. Google may provide the developer with aggregated, anonymous statistics (number of installs, system crash reports) under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask us for access, rectification, erasure, restriction or objection, and portability of your data by writing to madelatosoftware@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos).

Children

Ocho Puertas is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes any of the above, this page and its date will be updated before that version is published.
